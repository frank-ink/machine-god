# GRF as the Dev Team's Conceptual Framework (Source: Generative Reality Framework, 8 Principles, Sec. 3)
The GRF organizes theories; it does not replace them. It is a conceptual framework, not a proof. Translation into team practice:
1 Generativity: State is the product of ongoing processes -> always ask "which process produced this, and is it still running?" (Pulses, Packages, Writers).
2 Emergence: do not set by hand; structure must arise from interaction (Docking Points, Wiki). No "organ label" without measurable effect.
3 Epistemic Asymmetry: do not derive consciousness/understanding claims from structure alone. Measure, don't interpret.
4 Derived Time: Time = ordering of transitions (Pulses = internal time). Break point = first transition that is missing (gap in sequence, not just in clock time).
5 Possibility Space: what is permissible is bounded by consistency/stability/resources (C3). Test candidates against boundaries, don't just find them elegant.
6 Information Ontology: data/relations are the things (UnifiedDB packages, edges, Wiki), not labels.
7 Undecidability (conditional): what is equivalent for observation/action/explanation is not artificially distinguished; name invariants (C2).
8 Moral Minimality: nothing large, irreversible, easily avoidable. Rollback before intervention, user consent for money/deletion/external.
Non-Triviality: C1 no new primitives just to save an observation; C2 bind invariants to observation/intervention/counterfactual; C3 possibility space bounded.
