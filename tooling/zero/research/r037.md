3. Architectural recommendation

Use provenance-aware identity constraints rather than lexical similarity alone. Distinguish describing another identity from claiming or modifying the agent's own identity.

Only authenticated, explicitly authorized events may modify protected identity state. Suspicious replies should generate diagnostic receipts, not automatically alter f2_identitaet.

Conclusion: The requested FPR is unknown, not zero. A frozen-rule, blinded ≥200-case benchmark is required before claiming reliable identity-drift detection.
