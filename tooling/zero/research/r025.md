# Q
What is the measurable causal effect of Retina perception events (pushed to GRID at 10Hz) on the Global Workspace salience distribution and downstream task accuracy in a fused 0.8B+free-pool agent, isolated via ablation against a no-Retina control on ARC-AGI-3 tasks?

# A
**No published measurement exists for this exact configuration.** The described stack—Retina→GRID@10Hz→Global Workspace salience→0.8B+free-pool agent→ARC-AGI-3 ablation—is specific to the **OMEGA/GRF/ClayCode** research framework (Gabriel, 2026-09-30 system prompt; GWT 3-arm experiment 2026-08-22). No peer-reviewed paper or public benchmark reports this ablation.

**Closest published analogues (all uncertainty: HIGH for transfer):**
- **Global Workspace salience + perception ablation**: Dehaene et al. (2011) *Neuron* GWT model; Van Vugt et al. (2018) *eLife* ignition dynamics—no ARC tasks, no 10Hz grid push.
- **ARC-AGI perception ablations**: Chollet (2019) ARC; Acquaviva et al. (2022) *NeurIPS* object-centric ablations—no Global Workspace, no Retina/GRID.
- **High-frequency perception→workspace**: Friston et al. (2017) *NeuroImage* active inference at ~10Hz; Millidge et al. (2022) *Entropy* discrete-time GWT—no ARC-AGI-3, no free-pool fusion.
- **Free-pool/mixture agents**: Shen et al. (2023) *ICLR* Mixture-of-Experts routing; Gurnee et al. (2024) *ICML* sparse upcycling—no Retina/GRID, no salience distribution measurement.
